Course Syllabus & Structure

Clear learning paths across all age groups at Anokhi Paintings.

01. Warm-Up & Demo 20 Mins (Technique & Concept)
02. Guided Practice 30 Mins (Pencil / Stroke Drills)
03. Main Artwork 55 Mins (Canvas / Painting / Shading)
04. Review & Handwriting 15 Mins (Neatness & Feedback)

Module 1: Lines, Shapes & Grip

Straight, curved, and zigzag lines, basic shapes (circle, triangle, square), and foundational pencil grip control.

✍️ Free Handwriting Drills

Module 2: Primary Colours & Mixing

Introduction to the color wheel, warm & cool colors, rainbow blending, and playful poster color application.

🎨 Color Harmony Basics

Module 3: Cartoon Doodling & Animals

Drawing pets, birds, simple cartoon animals, and playful folk figures (Warli art) through simple shapes.

🐾 Cartoon & Observation

Module 4: Textures & Mini Canvas

Exploring brush loading, sponge textures, mixed media collage, and completing their first mini canvas artwork.

🖼️ Take-Home Canvas

Module 1: Drawing & Handwriting Foundations

Proportion, symmetry, object observation, alongside structured cursive/print handwriting discipline.

✍️ Free Handwriting Included

Module 2: Cartoon Characters & Shading

Designing cartoon expressions, transforming 2D shapes into 3D forms, and smooth light-to-dark values.

✏️ Pencil Shading & Cartoons

Module 3: Folk Art & Compositions

Detailed Madhubani motifs, underwater compositions, animals in motion, and perspective backgrounds.

🪶 Traditional & Modern Art

Module 4: Acrylic Canvas Mastery

Brushwork techniques, layering colors, handling acrylic paint, and creating exhibition-ready canvas paintings.

🎨 Canvas Board Painting

Module 1: Perspective & Architecture

Mastering 1-point and 2-point perspective, vanishing points, indoor spaces, and urban street sketching.

📐 Spatial Perspective

Module 2: Detailed Pencil Shading & Anatomy

Full 7-step value scales, facial proportions (eyes, nose, lips), hair textures, and still-life tonal studies.

✏️ Detailed Pencil Shading

Module 3: Advanced Acrylic & Caricature

Dry-brushing, glazing, atmospheric landscapes, character turnarounds, and high-contrast night scenes.

🖌️ Acrylic on Canvas

Module 4: Personal Project & Portfolio

Developing an original conceptual artwork from thumbnail sketch to final framed canvas, plus artist statement.

🏆 Portfolio & Showcase

Module 1: Choose Any Artwork You Like

Bring any painting, image, or reference that inspires you. We help you compose the layout and simplify the sketch.

🖼️ Choose Any Artwork

Module 2: Guided Freehand & Shading

Stress-free graphite shading, cartoon expressions, proportional sketching, and soft blending with stumps.

✏️ Pencil Shading & Tones

Module 3: Step-by-Step Acrylic Painting

Layering textures, color mixing, impasto, and completing a ready-to-frame floral, spiritual, or landscape painting.

🎨 Guided Acrylic Canvas

Module 4: Oil Painting & Classical Mediums

Glazing, fat-over-lean layering, rich oil color pigments, and preserving your finished canvas artwork.

🖌️ Classical Oil Mediums
